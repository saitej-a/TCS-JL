Frequently Answered Questions

Clear facts about how our community initiative functions and safeguards integrity.

help_outline

Is this officially linked to TCS?

No. TCS Joining Tracker is an entirely independent community project developed by incoming candidates and recent hires. It is not affiliated, endorsed, or authorized by Tata Consultancy Services Ltd.

verified

How do you verify letters without collecting private details?

We use an automated client-side hashing mechanism. Document verification timestamps and offer IDs are hashed into anonymous strings before hitting our database. When multiple candidates within the same college tier or geographical region report correlated dispatch dates, the wave status moves from 'Unconfirmed' to 'Verified'.

lock

Is my data anonymous?

Completely. We do not ask for your real name, email address, corporate employee ID, or phone number. You are assigned a randomly generated cryptographic handle upon participating, allowing you to update your milestone status without exposing your identity.

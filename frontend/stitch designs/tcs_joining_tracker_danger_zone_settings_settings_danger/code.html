Delete account permanently

Irreversible

Once deleted, your account cannot be recovered. Here is exactly what is destroyed:

  • Profile and timeline are deleted: All custom dates, offer letter milestones, and progress markers are wiped permanently.
  • Community posts stay but are shown as deleted author: Thread context remains useful to fellow candidates without your name or handle.
  • Survey responses are anonymised and kept for reporting: Zero-knowledge statistical averages will remain in collective cohort models.
  • Push subscriptions are removed from all devices: Browser service workers unregister and device keys are revoked.
Deletion runs after a 7-day grace period. Contact support within 7 days to cancel.

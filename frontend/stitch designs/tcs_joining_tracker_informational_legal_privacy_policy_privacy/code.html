Privacy Policy

This policy outlines how the TCS Joining Tracker community platform processes recruitment status updates, guarantees zero-knowledge pseudonymity, and safeguards candidate telemetry.

Independent Initiative Notice

TCS Joining Tracker is an independent, non-commercial community project operated by and for incoming candidates. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, authorized by, or officially connected to Tata Consultancy Services Limited (TCS) or any of its subsidiaries.

01

What we collect and why

We collect self-reported recruitment milestone dates (Offer Letter, Readiness Survey, Background Verification, and Joining Letter receipt) alongside non-identifying track metadata such as candidate cohort year and allocated location preference.

These data points are aggregated into regional dispatch velocity percentiles, enabling peers to estimate onboarding timelines accurately without disclosing corporate registration identifiers.

02

Email hashing and how your address is protected

Candidate email addresses are used strictly for passwordless authentication tokens and transactional account alerts. Your email is masked across all public interfaces and community feeds as a***@example.com.

Milestone verifications are paired with irreversible, one-way SHA-256 HMAC nonces. Even in the event of an infrastructure breach, raw candidate identities cannot be reversed or cross-indexed against corporate directories.

Complete Infrastructure Separation

This platform never interacts with official NextStep or Ultimatix portal APIs. We do not prompt for, collect, store, or transmit portal passwords, CT/DT reference numbers, or private internal credentials.

03

No third-party marketing trackers

We deploy zero commercial analytics SDKs, zero advertising telemetry pixels, and no behavioral tracking scripts. No Google Ads, Meta Pixel, or behavioral remarketing cookies are ever injected into your browser session.

Session storage and IndexedDB caches exist purely to sustain Progressive Web App (PWA) offline reading capabilities and preserve your local timeline draft while disconnected.

04

Notification token isolation

Browser WebPush subscriptions are stored as cryptographically signed endpoint keys. Push notifications are triggered only when a verified batch wave matching your stream (Digital, Ninja, or Prime) is reported in your geographic region.

You may independently revoke or renew browser push certificates at any time via Settings > Devices & Notifications without affecting your timeline milestones.

05

Your controls — identity mode, export and account anonymisation

You maintain full sovereignty over your public presence. Within your privacy configuration, you can toggle between three visibility modes: All verified candidates, My cohort only, or Nobody (full stealth mode).

Under Indian DPDP and global data portability principles, you can export a complete machine-readable JSON archive of your personal timeline history and forum submissions directly to your registered inbox.

06

How to request deletion

You can permanently purge your profile, timeline entries, device keys, and authentication credentials via the self-service Danger Zone tab in account settings.

Alternatively, write directly to the data privacy officer at hello@tjt-community.example using your registered email address. Requests are validated and executed within 7 business days.

Have a privacy concern or correction?

Our volunteer maintenance team reviews candidate security feedback promptly.

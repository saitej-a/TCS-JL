# TCS Joining Tracker — Design System (05_UI_UX_SPECIFICATION.md §4)

A trustworthy, calm, privacy-first recruitment-tracker SPA. Primary brand: indigo #4F46E5 (brand-600) with hover #4338CA and pressed #3730A3. Neutrals: Tailwind slate — light backgrounds #F8FAFC/#FFFFFF, dark backgrounds #0F172A/#1E293B. Semantic accents: emerald #10B981 (success/JOINED), amber #F59E0B (waiting/survey), sky #0EA5E9 (JL received), rose #EF4444 (danger). Typography: Inter everywhere; H1 28px bold tracking-tight, H2 22px semibold, body 15px/24px, captions 12px, badges 11px medium tracking-wide. Radii: rounded-lg (8px) controls, rounded-xl (12px) cards, rounded-2xl (16px) modals; pills fully rounded. Elevation: shadow-sm cards, shadow-xl modals. Both light AND dark themes are first-class (class-strategy dark mode); every surface must state both. Buttons: primary indigo w/ 2px offset focus ring, secondary slate, outline, ghost, danger rose; heights 32/40/48px; active:scale-[0.98]. Touch targets ≥44px on mobile. Footer on every screen carries the mandatory TCS non-affiliation disclaimer in 11px muted type.
